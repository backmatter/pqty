# Code of conduct

Participation in pqty's repositories, issue tracker, reviews, and other project
spaces must be respectful and harassment-free.

Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, assume good faith while
remaining open to correction, and make room for contributors with different
backgrounds and experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized attention, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement should
be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of interest where
practical.

Report routine problems to the maintainers in the relevant issue or pull
request. Submit sensitive or conduct-related reports through
[GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/backmatter/pqty/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `Conduct report:`. We use this form as a confidential
contact channel; vulnerability-specific fields are not required. If private
reporting is unavailable, open a public issue requesting a private channel
without including sensitive details.
