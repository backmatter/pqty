# Security policy

## Supported versions

Security fixes are developed on `main` and released in the latest version.
Older releases may require an upgrade.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not publish exploit details in an issue or discussion. Submit a report
through [GitHub's private vulnerability form](https://github.com/backmatter/pqty/security/advisories/new)
with:

- the affected version and platform;
- the smallest reproduction you can safely share;
- whether the input was a registry, archive, lock, trace, store, or output
  path;
- potential impact and known workarounds.

If private reporting is unavailable, open a public issue requesting a private
channel without including vulnerability details.

Maintainers will acknowledge a usable report, investigate it privately, and
coordinate a fix and disclosure appropriate to its impact.
